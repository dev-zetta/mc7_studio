Third-party native libraries in the hidapi 0.15.0 manylinux wheel

The release manifest records the exact SHA-256 digest and installed wheel path of every library listed here. The upstream versions that can be established from the auditwheel filenames are included. The wheel does not carry an upstream version record for every library.

libattr.so.1.1.0 contains libattr code from the attr project, Copyright 2000-2007 Silicon Graphics, Inc. and other contributors, under LGPL-2.1-or-later. Source: https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/jeffm/attr.git/

libelf.so and libdw.so are from elfutils 0.176, Copyright 1996-2019 Red Hat, Inc. and other contributors. The libraries are available under LGPL-3.0-or-later or GPL-2.0-or-later. Source: https://sourceware.org/elfutils/ftp/0.176/

libudev.so.1.6.2 is from systemd and is available under LGPL-2.1-or-later. Copyright includes Kay Sievers, Lennart Poettering and the systemd contributors. Source: https://github.com/systemd/systemd

liblzma.so.5.2.2 is from XZ Utils 5.2.2. The liblzma code in that release was placed in the public domain. Source: https://tukaani.org/xz/

libcap.so.2.22 is Copyright 1997-2011 Andrew G. Morgan and contributors and is available under the BSD 3-Clause license or GPL-2.0-only. Source: https://www.kernel.org/pub/linux/libs/security/linux-privs/libcap2/
